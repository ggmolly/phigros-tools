# Restart a song without loading

Restarting a song no longer goes through a loading screen: it starts again where you are. The HUD also counts your
judgements under the combo.

![](cover.svg)

## While you play

![](hud.webp)

- The grade and accuracy the play is heading for show under the score.
- A timing bar shows how early or late each hit was.
- Your perfects, goods, bads and misses count under the combo.

## Between plays

![](settings.webp)

- **Instant restart** starts the song again from the pause menu, or by holding pause, without loading.
- The **SKIP button** jumps over a song's intro or ending.
- The result screen shows the RKS the play gave you.
- Every one of these is a switch in the Phigros++ settings.
