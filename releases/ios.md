# Phigros++ on iPhone and iPad

Phigros++ now runs on iOS, with everything it has on Android. phigros.tools patches your copy of the game in your
browser, and nothing is uploaded.

![](cover.svg)

## Patched in your browser

![](patcher.webp)

- Choose your decrypted copy of Phigros on the [Phigros++ page](/phigrospp?device=ios): your browser turns it into
  `Phigros++.ipa` on your device.
- SideStore or AltStore installs it next to the game, signed with a free Apple ID.
- What it patches the game with is signed by the release key of Phigros++, and checked before anything is patched.
