# Rooms for up to 8 players

Everyone in a room plays the same chart at the same time, with each other's scores on screen as the song goes.

![](cover.svg)

## Open rooms

![](rooms.webp)

- The tab with two people on the chapter screen lists the open rooms, with the chart each one plays.
- **Create a room** makes yours. With a password only the players who type it join, and nothing it plays shows on
  phigros.tools.
- A room without a password shows live on [phigros.tools](/rooms) and keeps its matches there.

## In a room

![](room.webp)

- The first player is the host, under a golden crown, and starts the match once everyone is ready.
- Everyone plays at the same time, with each other's scores on screen as the song goes.
- Up to 8 more players can spectate, and watch any player's play live.
- Chat lines only go to the players in the room: they are never kept.
