# Play any chart with modifiers

Play any chart easier, harder, faster or in the dark. A Modifiers switch on the song list opens nine of them, each
with what it gives the score.

![](cover.svg)

## Nine ways to play a chart

![](modifiers.webp)

- **Easy** and **Hard Rock** widen or narrow the timing windows.
- **Half Time** and **Nightcore** play the song at 0.75× or 1.25×.
- **Hidden** fades the notes out before their line, and **Flashlight** only shows them near it.
- **Sudden Death** ends the song when the combo breaks, and **Perfect** starts it again at anything but a perfect.
- **Random** moves the notes to other lanes.

## What a play with modifiers counts for

![](song-list.webp)

- Nothing in the game: your save and records stay what the game itself gives.
- Its replay is kept, and your best with each set of modifiers goes to the chart's Modifiers leaderboard on
  phigros.tools.
- The total is the score times each modifier's multiplier.
