# Watch any play again

Every play is recorded and can be watched again, yours and the 50 best of each chart on phigros.tools. A trophy on
the chapter screen opens the players ranked by RKS.

![](cover.svg)

## Replays

![](replays.webp)

- The clock next to Play lists the chart's replays, laid out like the song list.
- A tap while watching shows a player: seek, pause, 5 seconds back or forward, and a speed from 0.25× to 16×.
- The seek bar marks every good, bad and miss where it happened.
- The game judges a replay exactly as it judged the play, so what you watch is what was played.

## Leaderboard

![](leaderboard.webp)

- The trophy lists the players of phigros.tools by RKS.
- **Verified only** counts the plays whose replay the site judged again.
