# Your profile on phigros.tools

Turn on **Sync to phigros.tools** in the game's Profile settings and your save, records and replays follow you to
[phigros.tools](/), under your Phigros name and a number of your own.

![](cover.svg)

## A profile that is yours

![](profile.webp)

- Your name gets a number, like `Name#0042`, and a page on phigros.tools to share.
- Your save goes after each song, the replay of each play that beats a record, and a summary of every song you finish.
- Each of these has its own switch, and **Unlisted profile** keeps you out of the leaderboards.

## 16 words and a code

![](code.webp)

- Turning sync on shows 16 words: they are the password of your profile, and bring it back on any device.
- **Sign in another device** shows a code that works once, for 10 minutes. Type it on the other device, or on
  phigros.tools to sign your browser in.
- **Sign out** forgets this device, and the Devices list on phigros.tools removes any other.
