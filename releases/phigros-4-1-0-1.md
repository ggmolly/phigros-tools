# Phigros++ runs on Phigros 4.1.0.1

Phigros++ runs on the newest Phigros from both stores: 4.1.0.1 on Google Play and 4.0.5 on the App Store, which is
the same game.

![](cover.svg)

## On Android

- Update Phigros to 4.1.0.1 from the Play Store, then open the Phigros++ installer and tap **Update**.
- Phigros 4.0.1.2 and 4.1.0 keep running Phigros++ if you haven't updated the game yet.

## On iPhone and iPad

- Patch your decrypted copy of Phigros 4.0.5 on the [Phigros++ page](/phigrospp?device=ios), then install the new
  `Phigros++.ipa` with SideStore or AltStore as before.
- Phigros 4.0.3 and 4.0.4 keep running Phigros++ if you haven't updated the game yet.
