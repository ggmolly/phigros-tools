# See what your friends are playing

Your friends now show in the game with the chart they are on, and a tap watches their play live. Rooms also keep a
queue of charts that anyone can add to.

![](cover.svg)

## Friends

![](friends.webp)

- Add a friend with their name and number, like `Name#0042`, or by tapping a player's row in a room.
- Friends who are playing show first with their chart, and tapping one watches their play live.
- A friend can invite you into their room. Tapping the toast joins it.
- **Appear invisible** shows you offline to everyone, and **Allow spectating** decides whether friends can watch you.

Your friends are also on [phigros.tools](/friends) once your browser is signed in to your profile.

![](toast.webp)

## A queue for every room

![](queue.webp)

- In a room, starting a song adds it to the queue, up to 20 charts and 3 from one player.
- The host orders the queue by holding a chart and dragging it to its place.
- Everyone plays the room's chart at their own level and with their own modifiers.
- The host can rename the room, or hand it over to another player.

## Fixes

- Moving a paused song no longer lets it run ahead of its music.
- The friend requests that came while the game was closed are told together at launch.
- Toasts are drawn like the game's own bars, at the bottom left.
